---
title: 'Reflections on Resurrection (2025)'
description: "Reflections on Bi Gan's epic science fiction drama Resurrection"
---

Bi Gan's *Resurrection* tells a classic and familiar story: when humanity discovers that the cause of pain, chaos, and mortality is the act of dreaming, we mostly give it up.
Except for at least one of us, who lives in a horrible cell, weeping and eating flowers dropped through a trapdoor by cavalier attendants.

This dreamer, played by [Jackson Yee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Yee), dies. And his death constitutes the entire action of the movie's narrative frame.

In his last few moments, he experiences 100 years.
